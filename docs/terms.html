Terms of use

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Lexora is a personal English-learning app made by Hatem Husam. These terms cover use of the app and this website.

The app

Lexora stores your words, sentences, blogs, reviews, and settings on your device. There is no Lexora account server. Signing in uses a local session on that device.

The vocabulary shipped for practice is original development content. It is not an official CEFR word list. A licensed catalog can replace it later.

Your use

Use Lexora for your own learning. Do not rely on it for exams, legal advice, medical advice, or news. You are responsible for the text you write in the app.

Deleting your account

Delete account in Settings erases your personal learning data and settings on that device and ends the local session. The shared vocabulary catalog stays on the device so the app can still open.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. Features can change. These pages are hosted for free on GitHub Pages.

Questions: github.com/Hatem48/lexora

حذف الحساب

حذف الحساب من الإعدادات يمسح بيانات التعلّم الشخصية والإعدادات على ذلك الجهاز وينهي الجلسة المحلية. كتالوج المفردات المشترك يبقى على الجهاز حتى يظل التطبيق قابلاً للفتح.
